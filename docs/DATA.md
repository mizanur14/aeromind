# Dataset provenance and handling

## Source

NASA C-MAPSS "Turbofan Engine Degradation Simulation Data Set", published by the NASA Ames
Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE).

- NASA Open Data Portal: <https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/cmapss-jet-engine-simulated-data>
- PCoE Data Set Repository: <https://www.nasa.gov/Intelligent-Systems-Division/Discovery-and-Systems-Health/Pcoe/Pcoe-Data-Set-Repository/>

**Cite the data as:**

- A. Saxena and K. Goebel (2008). "Turbofan Engine Degradation Simulation Data Set", NASA
  Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA.
- A. Saxena, K. Goebel, D. Simon, N. Eklund, "Damage Propagation Modeling for Aircraft Engine
  Run-to-Failure Simulation", *Proc. 1st Int. Conf. on Prognostics and Health Management
  (PHM08)*, Denver, CO, 2008. The paper is included with the download (`Damage Propagation Modeling.pdf`).

**What the data is and is not.**
- The trajectories were generated by the C-MAPSS **simulation** of a large commercial
  turbofan, with synthetic degradation and noise. They are not flight or test-cell data.
- NASA supplied the benchmark data. AeroMind is an independent project; NASA has not
  reviewed or endorsed it.
- The data.gov catalogue lists no explicit license. Follow NASA's terms on the pages above;
  AeroMind does not redistribute the files.

## Setup (once)

1. Download the C-MAPSS archive from one of the NASA pages above.
2. Extract it and place the 12 text files (`train_FD00x.txt`, `test_FD00x.txt`,
   `RUL_FD00x.txt`) plus `readme.txt` in `data/raw/cmapss/`. Optionally keep the outer
   archive at `data/raw/nasa_cmapss_outer.zip`.
3. Verify the files:

   ```bash
   uv run aeromind validate --dataset all    # must end with "Provenance: all hashes match"
   ```

   A hash mismatch means your copy differs from the one every reported result used. Do not
   overwrite `data/manifests/cmapss_provenance.json`; investigate the difference instead.

AeroMind never downloads data automatically. Without the files you can still run the tests
(real-data tests skip), `aeromind smoke`, `s2-smoke` and `s3-smoke`, and the dashboard's
report-based sections.

## Local layout (not committed)

```
data/raw/nasa_cmapss_outer.zip         outer archive (sha256 in data/manifests/nasa_cmapss_outer.sha256)
data/raw/cmapss/{train,test,RUL}_FD00{1..4}.txt, readme.txt
```

Put the 12 text files in `data/raw/cmapss/`. AeroMind never downloads or modifies them.
`data/raw/` is gitignored. Do not redistribute the data in this repository.

## Provenance

`data/manifests/cmapss_provenance.json` stores the SHA-256 and size of every file.
`uv run aeromind validate --dataset all` re-hashes the local files and fails on any mismatch.
Every training run also records the hashes of the train/test files it used. The test-label
hash is recorded only when `aeromind test` runs.

## Schema

26 whitespace-separated numeric columns: `engine_id`, `cycle`, `setting_1..3`, `sensor_1..21`.
Sensor symbols follow Saxena et al. (2008), Table 2 (see `aeromind.data.cmapss.SENSOR_INFO`;
for example `sensor_11` = Ps30, static pressure at the HPC outlet).

## Labels

- **Train:** run-to-failure; `rul = last_cycle − cycle` (0 at failure).
- **Test:** truncated; `RUL_FD00x.txt` row *i* is the RUL after the last observed cycle of
  test engine *i*; `rul(t) = last_cycle − t + RUL_i`.
- Engine IDs are local to each subset and file. They are never joined across subsets or
  between the train and test files of a subset: train engine 1 and test engine 1 are different engines.

## Known limitations

- `readme.txt` swaps the FD004 counts (says 248 train / 249 test) and says "26 sensors"
  instead of 21. The table above is measured.
- Simulated data with synthetic noise; results may not transfer to real fleets.
- FD001: sensors 1, 5, 10, 16, 18, 19 and setting 3 are constant; sensor 6 takes two values.
- FD001 core-speed sensors 9 (Nc) and 14 (NRc) trend in opposite directions for different
  engines (sign consistency 0.70 / 0.61 over training engines; see `reports/FD001/eda/train/within_engine_trends.csv`).
- The truncation rule for the test set is not documented.
